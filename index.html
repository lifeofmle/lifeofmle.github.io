Helping teams and users find clarity in a world of complexity

Dear friend,

When you look past the code and the pixels, you realize we are crafting digital tools to make it easier (ultimately better) for a person's life to interact with complex systems. Creating technology is, at its core, an act of human connection.

My journey began in the engineering trenches of New York and London, building real-time risk and pricing systems for investment banks. Those early days taught me how to navigate dense data and intricate architectures from the inside out. Yet the deeper I went, the more I noticed a disconnect: a system can meet every technical requirement, but it means nothing if it neglects the why of the person using it.

I wanted to focus on why someone would want to use what I was making.

That realization is what drove my transition to UX and product design. I wanted to solve for the human intent, not just the system. Since 2014, I have had the opportunity to design experiences across news publishing, hospitality, consumer applications, and the software development lifescycle. One thing for certain across all industries: complexity in a system is inevitable, but making that complexity invisible is the ultimate goal.

Now, we are entering a chapter of agentic workflows. Now, the boundaries between writing code and designing intent are blurring. Now, it is more important to understand human needs as technology augments more of how we work and think.

After spending decades thriving at the intersection of design, code, and human interaction. My experience has now become my unfair advantage to build the next user experiences.

Designer by craft. Creator by spirit. Leader by nature.

Scaling a design system across a platform

I lead a team of designers and front end developers at Kounta (now Lightspeed). We helped hospitality businesses operate efficiently by shaping and building the software they use everyday.

From a design perspective, the product rebrand involved us translating the brand vision into product so that it elevates the experience for our customers.

From this project, along with my coding experience and love of HTML/CSS, I went on to lead the design system (React) between the design and engineering teams for delivering a consistent experience for our customers.

Simplifying product through discovery

One of the most common queries for online delivery services is "Where is my order?"

I ran workshops and research to identify opportunities for the team to improve on in the service.

The team was about to start work on a big piece of work but as we did more research we discovered flaws in what we thought the user knew. This lead us to pivot on our direction to make our final solution more usable

The moral of this story is to continually get your design in front of your customers so you can get better context.
